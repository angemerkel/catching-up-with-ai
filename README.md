# Catching up with AI

*Catching up with AI* is a personal learning notebook documenting my exploration of generative AI, from the foundations of machine learning and neural networks to working with AI assistants, AI-assisted coding and the use of AI in scientific research.

The project grew out of an attempt to understand AI beyond the hype: how it works, what it can and cannot do, how to work with it effectively, and where it might become useful in computational and scientific work.

The rendered notebook is available at:

[**https://angemerkel.github.io/catching-up-with-ai/**](https://angemerkel.github.io/catching-up-with-ai/){.uri}

## Contents

The notebook is organised into five broad areas:

1.  **Foundations** — Machine learning, neural networks and the concepts behind modern generative AI.
2.  **Working with AI** — Prompt engineering, working with data and documents, human–AI workflows, and verification.
3.  **Building with AI** — Configured AI assistants, AI-assisted coding, grounding models with data, and AI-enabled workflows.
4.  **AI for Science** — Literature discovery, scientific reasoning, data analysis, scientific communication, and the use of AI as a research tool.
5.  **Responsible AI** — Limitations, risks, ethical considerations, governance and legal frameworks.

A separate **Notebook** contains shorter observations, experiments and updates that do not necessarily form part of the main chapters.

The material in `notes/` is working material and is not part of the published notebook. Once material develops into 
a chapter or notebook entry, the corresponding `.qmd` file becomes the version that is maintained. 

## Development 

The site is written in [Quarto]() and published through GitHub Pages. 

My general workflow is: 

**learn / read / experiment → take notes → organise and write → preview and revise → render → commit** 

The notebook is intentionally developed incrementally. Individual pages are dated according to when I originally worked 
through the material, while later observations and corrections can be added separately rather than continually rewriting 
earlier notes. 

## Use of AI 

Generative AI is both the subject of this project and one of the tools I use while developing it. I use AI assistants to discuss 
concepts, explore questions, review structure and wording, and support some coding and technical tasks. The ideas, final selection, 
interpretation and organisation of the material remain my own. Where appropriate, the courses, papers and other resources on which 
individual sections are based are referenced directly in the text.
